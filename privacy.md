## 中文

### 概要

「在场」是一款在设备本地记录演出经历的私人手账 App。当前版本不要求注册或登录，不提供云账户，不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK，也不会把演出记录、回收站内容或偏好设置上传给开发者。

### App 处理的数据

App 会根据你的操作在设备上保存：

- 你录入或编辑的演出记录，例如艺人、日期、城市、场馆、评分、标签、日记、票价和座位信息；
- 你移入回收站的演出记录；
- App 偏好设置，例如语言、主题和分享水印设置。

这些数据用于提供记录、搜索、统计、地图展示、分享卡和恢复功能。当前版本将重要数据保存在 iOS 原生本地存储中，并保留一个供现有界面使用的本地镜像。开发者无法远程读取这些数据。

### 照片和视频

当前版本允许你拍摄或从系统选择器选择照片和视频，并将你确认保存的媒体存入 App 的本地文件目录、关联到相应演出记录。App 不会把这些媒体上传给开发者。

### 本地备份与恢复

你可以主动导出版本化 JSON 备份文件。备份包含演出记录、回收站记录、偏好设置，以及记录所引用的照片和视频。包含媒体的备份可能较大。文件只会在你选择系统分享或“文件”保存位置时离开 App；App 不会自动上传备份。

从文件恢复时，App 会先验证文件并显示影响预览。只有你确认后，备份内容才会替换当前设备上的对应数据。

### 网络与第三方服务

当前正式构建不依赖远程字体或 CDN，不包含广告、行为分析、崩溃分析、账户或云同步服务。系统分享面板、文件选择器及你主动选择的第三方分享目的地由 Apple 或相应服务提供方依其政策处理。

### 保留、删除与控制

- 记录会保留在设备上，直到你删除、从回收站彻底删除、恢复其他备份，或卸载 App；
- 卸载 App 通常会删除其本地数据；
- 你导出的备份文件由你自行管理，可在“文件”或相应保存位置删除；
- 当前版本没有服务器账户，因此没有开发者持有的云端账户数据可供删除。

在清除设备、卸载 App 或更换设备前，请先导出备份。

### 儿童隐私

App 不会有意收集儿童的个人信息，也没有面向儿童的账户、广告或追踪功能。

### 政策变更

如果未来加入会员账户、云备份、跨设备同步、分析服务或媒体持久化，本政策会在相关功能上线前更新，并在需要时征求你的同意。

### 联系方式

如对隐私、数据或删除方式有疑问，请联系：[iwasthere.support@gmail.com](mailto:iwasthere.support@gmail.com)。

## English

### Summary

Iwasthere is a private, on-device journal for live events. The current version does not require an account, provide cloud storage, include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, or upload your event records, recently deleted items, or preferences to the developer.

### Data Handled by the App

The app stores event records you create or edit, recently deleted records, and app preferences on your device. This data powers the journal, search, statistics, map, share cards, and restore features. Important data is stored in native iOS local storage with a local compatibility mirror. The developer cannot remotely access it.

### Photos and Videos

The current version lets you capture or select photos and videos through iOS system interfaces. Media that you confirm and save is stored in the app's local files and linked to the relevant event record. It is not uploaded to the developer.

### Local Backup and Restore

You may export a versioned JSON backup containing event records, recently deleted records, preferences, and referenced photos and videos. Backups that contain media may be large. A backup leaves the app only when you choose a destination through the system share or Files interface; the app does not upload it automatically. Imports are validated and previewed before confirmed data replacement.

### Network and Third Parties

The production build does not depend on remote fonts or CDNs and contains no advertising, behavioral analytics, crash analytics, account, or cloud-sync service. Apple and any destination you select in a system share sheet or file picker process that action under their own policies.

### Retention, Deletion, and Control

Records remain on the device until you delete them, permanently remove them from Recently Deleted, restore another backup, or uninstall the app. Exported backup files remain under your control. Because the current version has no server account, the developer holds no cloud account data to delete. Export a backup before uninstalling, erasing, or replacing your device.

### Changes and Contact

This policy will be updated before any future account, membership cloud backup, cross-device sync, or analytics feature is launched. For privacy or data questions, contact [iwasthere.support@gmail.com](mailto:iwasthere.support@gmail.com).
